# Full-color images suited to six-color T-shirt printing

These images were selected for a clear focal subject, recognizable silhouette,
strong tonal separation, and broad color families that should remain attractive
after reKolor reduces them to six Pantone colors. All local copies are JPEG or
PNG and have been resized to keep interactive processing practical.

## Shirt and palette notes

- For a **light or natural shirt**, start with the Wave, macaw, or flamingo.
  Their pale paper can visually merge into the garment.
- For a **black or navy shirt**, start with the tiger, astronaut, sunset,
  jellyfish, or Starry Night. In production, the garment can replace the
  darkest ink, leaving room for another accent color.
- The tiger PNG has transparency. Choose the shirt's color as the material:
  reKolor leaves the material's own color unprinted, so the transparent
  background (and dark areas within its ΔE on a black shirt) takes no ink and
  is transparent in the downloaded PNG.
- Treat six colors as six broad tonal roles, not six tiny sampled details:
  deepest shadow, dark midtone, light midtone, highlight, warm accent, and cool
  accent. Pick colors from large important regions of the subject.
- Avoid selecting tiny highlights first. reKolor maps every pixel to the nearest
  selected Pantone color, so a balanced set spanning dark-to-light values gives
  better structure than six saturated accents.

## Best first trial

Use `04-tiger.png` on a black shirt and select dark brown, medium brown, orange,
gold, cream, and white. Let the shirt supply black in the final screen-print
separation. For a light shirt, use `01-great-wave.jpg` and select the darkest
navy, medium blue, pale blue, charcoal-gray, tan, and cream.
