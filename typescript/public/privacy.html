Privacy

reKolor · last updated 9 October 2026

Your images stay on your device

reKolor works in your browser. The images and palette files you open are processed on your device; the app doesn't upload them anywhere.

So that reloading the page doesn't lose your work, the app keeps the open image, your picks, the material, the unprinted colors and an uploaded palette in your browser's storage for this tab (session storage and IndexedDB). They stay on your device, and once you close the tab they are deleted (the image the next time you open the app).

This site records visits

To find bugs and see how the app is used, this site (methanoya.github.io/reKolor) records each visit with LogRocket, a session-replay service run by LogRocket, Inc., in the United States. Recording starts when the page opens.

What is recorded

  • The page as you see it, and what you do on it: clicks, scrolling, and the settings you change (such as the material color and the inks), except the parts listed below.
  • Messages in the browser's console, errors, and performance data such as load times.
  • Your browser's details: its type and version, operating system, screen size, language, and the page you came from.
  • A few named events, with numbers only: an image opened (its format, size in pixels and number of colors), picks added, removed or cleared, palettes imported or exported, a PNG downloaded (how many picks and inks), and an ink palette uploaded, downloaded or reset to Pantone (how many inks). And the kind of any error the app shows, such as "the browser couldn't decode the file", without its message.

What is not recorded

  • Your images, and the previews made from them.
  • The names of your files, an uploaded ink palette's included: the parts of the page that show them are left out.
  • Your IP address: this site tells LogRocket not to store it.
  • Network requests.

The parts of the page that are left out never reach LogRocket. Recordings aren't linked to a name or an account (the site has none), and they aren't used for advertising.

How long, and who sees them

Recordings are kept for the retention period of this site's LogRocket plan, then deleted. Only the site's maintainer can view them, in LogRocket, which handles them on the site's behalf (see LogRocket's privacy policy).

Not being recorded

A content or tracker blocker that blocks LogRocket stops the recording; the app works the same without it. A copy of reKolor you run yourself, from its source code, records nothing.

Questions and deletion

Open an issue at github.com/methanoya/reKolor/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details. To have a recording deleted, give the date and approximate time of your visit, and your browser.
